查询: What are the concerns surrounding the AMOC?

--- 句子窗口检索结果 ---
回答: There is low confidence in understanding the changes to the Atlantic meridional overturning circulation (AMOC) that occurred during the 20th century due to a lack of agreement between quantitative reconstructed and simulated trends. Additionally, directobservational records have been too short to determine the relative contributions of internal variability, natural forcing, and anthropogenic forcing to observed changes.

Looking toward the future, while it is highly likely that the circulation will decline across all scenarios, there is low confidence in the quantitative specifics of these projections. However, it is unlikely that an abrupt collapse will occur before 2100.

--- 常规检索结果 ---
回答: Concerns regarding the Atlantic overturning circulation (AMOC) largely stem from uncertainties in its historical changes and the causes behind them. There is currently low confidence in both reconstructed and modelled AMOC changes for the 20th century. Additionally, observational records since the mid-2000s have been too short to determine the relative contributions of internal variability, natural forcing, and anthropogenic forcing to AMOC change. While a decline is projected over the 21st century, the quantitative projections of this decline remain low confidence.
